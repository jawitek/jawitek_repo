KAP CO | Brand GuidelinesStrategy
01

Brand strategy

KAP CO packs and mills everyday dry staples: rice, groats, beans and lentils, and flours made from them. It is the KAP Holding company closest to the family's roots — food production — and the first one whose product sits on a kitchen shelf.

Positioning

A family-owned packer and miller of dry staples. Reliable origin, clean product, honest weight — supplied to retail chains, wholesalers and food producers with the flexibility of a company where decisions are made in one room.

Main message

Staples, done properly.

Supporting messages

Traceable origin for every lot. Cleaned, sorted and packed in Poland. Flours milled from the same grain and pulses we pack, so one supplier covers the whole range.

Proof points

Capital and management in the hands of one family since the 1970s. Food-production heritage: the Konspol project and the KONSDROB poultry business. The KAP group's four decades of business track record stand behind every delivery.

Brand persona

The family keywords carry over — authority, security, partnership — with three words that only a food company needs.

#origin#security#quality#partnership#everyday#authority#transparency

Audience

Category buyers at retail chains, wholesalers, food producers using flours as ingredients, and, on the pack, the household cook.

Tone

Plain and exact. Weights, origins and lot numbers are part of the brand voice, not small print. No superlatives, no "premium".

KAP CO | Brand GuidelinesThe KAP family
02

One system, one more company

Every KAP company shares the same construction: KAP in Helvetica Bold, the company name in Helvetica Light, both in Dark Green, and the corner bracket in a colour that belongs to that company alone. KAP CO takes the bracket in Harvest Gold, and is the one member whose O carries a mark: a barley ear growing through it.

Why "CO"

Two letters, like RE, IN and VC. It reads as commodities and as company, and it keeps the lockup the same width class as its siblings.

Why a gold bracket

The family already owns red, orange, lime and purple. Harvest Gold is the colour of a ripe field and of the ear itself, so the bracket and the ear share one colour and read as one mark.

Where KONSDROB fits

KONSDROB is an operating brand with its own wordmark, but it shares the green and the Helvetica pairing. KAP CO answers KONSDROB's bird with a barley ear in its O (chapter 03). Both companies put the product inside the letter, in their own way.

03

Logotype

The primary logo is the two-colour version on white. Use the negative version on Dark Green and on photography; use the monochrome version only where colour is not available (engraving, single-colour print, fax-grade documents).

Construction

All dimensions are multiples of the cap height H. The letterforms, the spacing between KAP and the suffix, and the bracket's size and offset are identical to KAP RE, KAP IN and KAP VC; only the suffix letters and the bracket colour change. The ear is centred on the O, its top level with the bracket top, and the O is cut away around it so the mark works on any background.

Never rebuild the logo from live text. Use the SVG or PNG masters in chapter 08; Helvetica Neue is not installed on most machines, and a substituted font changes the widths.

Safe area

Keep a clear space of one cap height (H) on every side, measured from the bracket top and from the foot of the ear. Nothing enters this area: no text, no other logos, no edge of the page.

Minimum size

The family minimum is 35 mm wide in print, 120 px on screen. Below that the ear's grains merge, so use the simple version without the ear down to 20 mm / 70 px, and the bracket alone below that.

Business card, labels

20–35 mm: simple version
Favicon / app icon: bracket only

With descriptor

For contexts where "CO" needs explaining: first-contact documents, trade fair walls, the website footer. Never on packaging, where the product name does the explaining.

Simple version, without the ear

For sizes under 35 mm, embroidery, engraving and single-colour stamps, where the ear's grains would merge into a blot. Same letters, same bracket; the O is whole. Never use it where the full logo fits.

Incorrect use
Do not stretch or condense.
Do not rotate; the bracket is the only angled reading.
Do not recolour the letters or move the bracket to a sibling's colour.
Do not place the primary version on low-contrast or busy backgrounds; use negative.
Do not drop the ear where the full logo fits; the simple version is for small sizes only.
Do not enlarge the bracket or the ear; both are locked to the cap height.

05

Typography

Helvetica Neue, as in every KAP company: Light for large headings and product names, Regular for running text, Bold for emphasis and the KAP of the logotype. Arial is the auxiliary face for e-mail, spreadsheets and anything typed on a machine without Helvetica.

Front panel anatomy
  1. Logotype top left, Dark Green on light grounds, negative with Harvest Gold bracket and ear on Black Bean and Moss. Width: 40–45 % of the panel.
  2. Product group top right in small caps, Polish and English.
  3. Product name in Helvetica Neue Light, Polish first, then the English name and origin in one line.
  4. Window on the right: the product is the picture. No photography of fields or bowls on the front.
  5. Net weight with the ℮ mark, bold, bottom right, and the lot number bottom left: honest weight is the brand promise, so it is set as a feature.
Back panel
  1. Nutrition table per 100 g in a bordered box on white, as required by Regulation (EU) 1169/2011.
  2. Origin, best-before and lot repeated as a block, never split across seams.
  3. Producer line: KAP CO sp. z o.o., address, and the KAP Holding endorsement in Regular, one size below.

Stationery
